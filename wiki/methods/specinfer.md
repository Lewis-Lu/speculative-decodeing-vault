# SpecInfer

ASPLOS'24 serving system: small models grow a **token tree**; LLM verifies with tree-based parallel decoding. 1.5–2.8× distributed, 2.6–3.5× offload. Ancestor of the tree-verify idea used by Medusa/EAGLE.
